Release notes

Psion took shape across five years. One song was written in the isolation of 2020, at the height of the pandemic, when everything felt unreal. The others grew from riffs written in spring 2023. The vocals and drums were completed during the week before release.

The record moves quickly without becoming careless. Spoken word unravels into screaming; melodic foundations collapse into dissonance. The rawness is deliberate, and the dense layering gives emotional catharsis a physical form.

Psion emerged from recognizing my own emotional patterns and the ways I contributed to the damage I believed was being done to me. The songs carry that recognition without pretending it brought resolution. What first appeared to be a fight with the outside world was always internal.
