ucygrx

Human signal, machine form.

ucygrx is Gabriel Long’s electronic project, exploring the strange emotional connection between humans and machines. Across six releases, repetition opens into a psychological space that feels physical, unstable, nostalgic, and unmistakably human. The catalog runs from the hand-performed loops of Stigmatech to nadir, a one-hour single-take work used in a thesis presentation on adolescent mental health, bullying exposure, and predictive modeling.

The Longtooth Collective

The Imitation Zone

The Longtooth Collective was a volatile meeting point in the Bay Area underground, bringing together angular guitar, forceful bass, searching drums, and three vocalists. Recorded in 2008, The Imitation Zone captures the group’s songs as collisions of structure, impulse, and shared intent.

Members went on to form Kowloon Walled City, Tigon, Early Graves, Less Art, Ex Everything, and beareab.
