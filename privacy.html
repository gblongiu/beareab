Privacy and analytics

Listening and downloading never require tracking. Analytics runs only with your permission, and your choice never changes access.

On this page

What is measured What is not collected Your choice Measurement limits

Google Analytics remains off unless you explicitly allow it.

What is measured

If you allow analytics, the site records aggregate visits and a limited set of interactions: starting a track, listening for 30 seconds, completing a track, opening lyrics, choosing a download, and following the support link to PayPal.

Reports may also include an approximate region, device type, referring page, and pages visited. Google may set a first-party _ga cookie to distinguish one browser from another.

What is not collected

This site does not intentionally send Google Analytics your name, email address, PayPal details, contribution amount, message contents, or downloaded files. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in the site configuration.

Analytics is used only to understand visits, listening, download interest, and traffic to the support page.

Your choice

Declining analytics changes nothing about the catalog, player, lyrics, downloads, or support page. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, analytics stays off automatically.

Checking this device’s analytics preference.

Measurement limits

A download event records a click, not a completed transfer. A PayPal event records a visit to PayPal, not a completed contribution. Only PayPal can confirm a completed contribution.

Consent choices, privacy tools, ad blockers, private browsing, and cleared cookies all reduce the totals. The resulting numbers are estimates, not a census.
