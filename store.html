Apparel, prints & objects

A small line of heavyweight apparel, prints, posters, stickers, hats, and embroidered objects is being developed from the release artwork.

Nothing will be offered until the materials, print method, artwork placement, and final presentation have been reviewed.

How future orders will work

Different specialist partners may manufacture and ship different items. Checkout will take place on the partner’s clearly identified secure site.

Made after purchase

On-demand items enter production after an order is placed, so dispatch takes longer than with warehoused stock.

Preorder editions

A conventionally pressed edition may depend on a funding threshold. Any deadline, progress, cancellation terms, and estimated production window will appear before purchase.

Shipping & taxes

The fulfillment partner calculates shipping, taxes, available destinations, and delivery estimates during checkout.

Separate parcels

Music, apparel, and prints may arrive separately when different partners make them. The partner named on the product listing and receipt provides order support.
