Artist profile

Gabriel Long

Gabriel Long writes, performs, and produces music as beareab.

Biography

Sound as a way into what stays hidden.

Gabriel Long is the musician, songwriter, and producer behind beareab. Born in the Pacific Northwest and raised in the Midwest, he began writing and playing guitar in 2000, then started releasing music as beareab in 2025. The project draws from underground hardcore and experimental music, binding exposed emotion to intricate, abrasive guitar work.

Across Spectacle, Psion, the beareab / eyesplice split, and Solace, Long traces hauntings, memory, shadow integration, and self-therapy. Dissonant riffs, volatile dynamics, and fragile melody make those interior subjects physical.

Approach

Whether recording alone at home or collaborating in the underground scene, Long works within a DIY practice shaped by audio production and recording since 2000. He values immediacy, captured performance, and imperfection that serves the song over polish for its own sake.

Long also records electronic music as ucygrx and performed vocals with The Longtooth Collective.
